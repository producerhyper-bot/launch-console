Frequently asked questions

Straight answers — no marketing spin.

Where do the fees actually go?
The base fee and any security-feature fees you select are sent in one transaction to the platform's fee wallet, shown on the Review step with a copyable address and QR code. If you also add initial liquidity, that SOL and those tokens go straight from your wallet into the Raydium pool in a separate transaction — they never touch the platform wallet. You'll always see the full breakdown before you sign anything.
Is this custodial? Do I have to hand over my keys?
No. You connect your own Phantom wallet and approve every transaction individually — the fee payment, the mint, the liquidity deposit, and any sell you make later. Nothing is ever signed on your behalf, and your private keys never leave your wallet.
Can the platform freeze my tokens or drain holder wallets later?
No such function exists in this app, and it isn't planned. By default a newly minted SPL token's mint and freeze authorities sit with your connected wallet, same as any token you'd create yourself — the platform never holds them. The optional "Revoke" toggles let you permanently give those authorities up on-chain if you want holders to be able to verify that.
What's the difference between Devnet and Mainnet?
Devnet is Solana's public test network — tokens and SOL there aren't real and cost nothing, so it's the safe place to try the full flow first. Mainnet is the live network: deploying there spends real SOL and creates a real, irreversible on-chain token. Use the toggle near the top of this page to switch, and double-check which one is selected before you sign.
Where do the price and liquidity numbers on the dashboard come from?
Directly from DexScreener's public API once your pool is indexed, and swap quotes come from Jupiter, Solana's standard aggregator. Nothing on the dashboard is simulated — if a number looks off, it's reflecting real on-chain state, not a display bug.
Do I need the AI name/icon generator to use this?
No — it's optional. If the server doesn't have the AI keys configured, the buttons show an error and you just fill in the name, symbol, description, and icon by hand, same as any other field.
Is creating a token the same as it succeeding as an investment?
No. This tool handles the technical side of minting a real SPL token and, optionally, seeding a liquidity pool for it. Whether anyone else buys, holds, or trades it afterward is entirely up to the market — nothing here manufactures demand, volume, or holders. Token launches carry real financial risk; only spend what you can afford to lose, and treat this page as infrastructure, not financial advice.
Launch Console mints real SPL tokens on Solana. It does not, and will not, simulate trading activity, fake volume, or provide a way to freeze or drain holder funds after launch. Deploying is a real, irreversible, on-chain action once confirmed on mainnet. Post-launch price and liquidity stats come straight from DexScreener's public API, and selling routes through Jupiter — every number on this page reflects real on-chain state, and every sell requires your explicit wallet approval.
